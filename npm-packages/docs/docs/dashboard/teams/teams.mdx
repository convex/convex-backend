In Convex, your projects are organized by team. Teams are used to share access
to your projects with other people. To switch between teams or create a new
team, click your team's name at the top of the Convex dashboard. Inside a
project, the same menu is behind your team's icon, to the left of the project
name.

<Screenshot story="docs/pages/Projects#Team Switcher" alt="Team switcher" />

The menu lists every team you belong to, and typing filters it. Pressing `/` or
`⌘K` (`Ctrl+K` on Windows and Linux) anywhere in the dashboard opens the command
palette, which has the same **Switch Team** menu alongside search across your
projects and deployments.

You may change the name of a team or invite new members to a team by clicking on
the "Team Settings" button located on the top of the project list page.

## General

The [general page](https://dashboard.convex.dev/team/settings) allows changing
the team name, slug, and the [default region](/production/regions.mdx) for new
deployments. Each setting can be edited and saved independently.

This page also displays your team ID, which is used to identify your team when
working with the [Convex Platform APIs](/platform-apis/overview.mdx).

You may also delete the team from this page. You can only delete a team after
deleting all of it's projects, and removing all other team members from your
team. Deleting your team will automatically cancel your Convex subscription.

### Roles and permissions

Convex has two levels of control for managing access to your team, projects, and
deployments. Team-level roles control what a user can do within the team, while
project-level permissions control what a user can do within a specific project.

#### Team roles

Your team members can have one of the following roles:

- Admin
- Developer

The creator of the team is automatically assigned the Admin role. When inviting
new team members, you may select a role for them. You may also change the role
of a team member at any time.

Developers can:

- Create new projects and deployments. When a new project is created, the
  creator of the project is automatically granted the
  [Project Admin](#project-admins) role for that project.
- View existing projects, and create development and preview deployments for
  these projects. Developers may read data from production deployments, but
  cannot write to them.
- View the team's usage and billing status (such as previous and upcoming
  invoices)

Admins can do everything developers can, as well as:

- Invite new team members
- Remove members from the team
- Change the role of other team members
- Manage the team's Convex subscription and billing details.
- Change the team name, slug, and default region
- Team Admins are also implicitly granted project admin access to all projects
  within the team. See [Project Admins](#project-admins) for more information.

#### Project Admins

In addition to team roles, you may also grant admin access to individual
projects by granting team members the "Project Admin" role.

If you are a Project Admin for a given project, you may:

- Update the project name and slug
- Update the project's default environment variables
- Delete the project
- Write to production deployments

You may assign and remove the Project Admin role for multiple projects at the
same time on the member settings page. To assign or remove the Project Admin
role for multiple members at the same time, visit the
[Project Settings](/dashboard/projects.mdx#project-settings) page instead.

#### Custom Roles

On Convex Business, you can define
[custom roles](/team-management/custom-roles.mdx) with fine-grained permissions
that go beyond the built-in Admin and Developer roles. A team member is assigned
either a built-in role or one or more custom roles. Project Admin access is
independent and applies on top of either choice.

For the full action list and which built-in roles cover which actions, see
[Role Actions](/team-management/role-actions.mdx).

## Billing

Use the [billing page](https://dashboard.convex.dev/team/settings/billing) to
upgrade your Convex subscription to a higher tier, or manage your existing
subscription.

On paid plans, you can also update your billing contact details, payment method,
and view your invoices.

[Learn more about Convex pricing](https://www.convex.dev/pricing).

### Spending limits

When you have an active Convex subscription, you can set the spending limits for
your team on the
[billing page](https://dashboard.convex.dev/team/settings/billing):

- The **warning threshold** is only a soft limit: if it is exceeded, the team
  will be notified by email, but no other action will be taken.
- The **disable threshold** is a hard limit: if it is exceeded, all projects in
  the team will be disabled. This will cause errors to be thrown when attempting
  to run functions in your projects. You can re-enable projects by increasing or
  removing the limit.

Spending limits only apply to the resources used by your team's projects beyond
the amounts included in your plan. The seat fees (the amount paid for each
developer in your team) are not counted towards the limits. For instance, if you
send the spending limit to $0/month, you will be billed for the seat fees only
and the projects will be disabled if you exceed the built-in resources included
in your plan.

## Referrals

Use the [referrals page](https://dashboard.convex.dev/team/settings/referrals)
to refer friends to Convex and earn free resources. For each team you refer (up
to 5), both your team and the referred team receive additional resources on top
of the [free plan limits](https://www.convex.dev/pricing).

Referral benefits only apply while your team is on the free, though your
referral link keeps working for the friends you refer. Share the referral link
found on this page to get started, and you'll also see who referred you and
which teams you've referred.

## Team Authentication

<Admonition type="info">

Single Sign-On and Directory Sync are only available on Convex Business and
Enterprise.

</Admonition>

The
[team authentication page](https://dashboard.convex.dev/team/settings/team-authentication)
is where you verify the email domains your team owns and connect your
organization's identity provider:

- **[Single Sign-On (SSO)](/team-management/sso.mdx)** lets team members
  authenticate through your identity provider.
- **[Directory Sync (SCIM)](/team-management/directory-sync.mdx)** provisions
  members and their roles from your identity provider's user directory.

## Audit Log

<Admonition type="info">

The Audit Log is only available on Convex Professional, Business, and Enterprise
plans.

</Admonition>

The [audit log page](https://dashboard.convex.dev/team/settings/audit-log) shows
all the actions taken by members within the team. This includes creating and
managing projects and deployments, inviting and removing team members, and more.
If your team has [Single Sign-On](/team-management/sso.mdx#sign-in-audit-logs)
enabled, the audit log also includes SSO sign-ins to the dashboard and CLI.

<Screenshot story="docs/pages/TeamAuditLog" alt="Team audit log page" />

You may also view a history of deployment-related events on the
[deployment history page](/dashboard/deployments/history.mdx).

## Access Tokens

Use the
[access tokens page](https://dashboard.convex.dev/team/settings/access-tokens)
to create team access tokens for the
[Convex Platform APIs](/platform-apis/overview.mdx).

A team access token can only perform actions you have access to on the team. If
you have [custom roles](/team-management/custom-roles.mdx), the token can only
perform the actions allowed by your roles. You can only see and manage the
tokens you have created yourself.

## Applications

The [applications page](https://dashboard.convex.dev/team/settings/applications)
has two tabs:

- **Authorized Applications** lists the third-party applications you have
  authorized, via OAuth, to access this team on your behalf. You can revoke an
  application's access here at any time. Like team access tokens, an authorized
  application can only perform actions you have access to on the team, and you
  can only see the applications you authorized yourself.
- **Your OAuth Applications** lets you create and manage OAuth applications so
  that other Convex users can authorize your product to manage their teams or
  projects. See [OAuth Applications](/platform-apis/oauth-applications) for
  details.
